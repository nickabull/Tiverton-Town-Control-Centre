Around the League
Dorchester go top as comeback wins, late drama and a first league victory shape the weekend.
Saturday 3 October 2026

New leaders: Swindon Supermarine 0–1 Dorchester Town

Dorchester climbed to the top after Ilya Ihnatenko struck in the 77th minute, moments after coming on. Supermarine slipped to fourth.

Five straight: Bishops Cleeve 3–0 Melksham Town

Aaron Evans-Harriott, Jamal Lawrence and Adulai Sambu were on target as Cleeve made it five wins in a row. Both teams finished with ten men.

Inkberrow hit back: Sporting Club Inkberrow 3–1 Shaftesbury

Shaftesbury led through Cameron Murray, but Connor Stanley levelled before Gregor Guy and Joshua Hurdman completed the comeback.

Late joy for Falmouth: Falmouth Town 2–1 Larkhall Athletic

Milo Murgatroyd put Larkhall ahead, Jack Webber equalised before half-time and Oscar Massey won it for Falmouth in the 86th minute.

Thomas turns it around: Westbury United 2–1 Exmouth Town

Matthew Wright gave Exmouth the lead, but Leighton Thomas scored twice after the break to swing the game Westbury's way.

Four in the first half: Tiverton Town 2–2 Worcester Raiders

Worcester led twice through Prince Henry and Stan Asomugha, with Aiden Horne and Finn Roberts replying for Tivvy in an action-packed opening 45 minutes.

Portland steal it: Bideford 1–2 Portland United

Jordan Ewing put Bideford in front before Jacob Coombs levelled, and Harley Brook grabbed a dramatic 93rd-minute winner for Portland.

Hartpury leave it late: Hartpury 2–1 Barnstaple Town

Oscar Knight gave Barnstaple an early lead, Louis Manning equalised and Billy Osbourn won it in the 91st minute. Barnstaple later saw Brodie Montague dismissed.

First league win: Slimbridge 1–2 Bristol Manor Farm

Mikey Morgan and Joe Beardwell put Manor Farm two up and they held on despite a late Joseph Stokes goal for Slimbridge.
