Use BA1 8DJ for Plain Ham.

A motorway option from Tiverton is M5 north, M4 east, then J18 and the A46/A4 into Larkhall. Let the live route planner choose the best route on the day.

The final approach

From the A4, use St Saviour’s Road and Larkhall Square toward Charlcombe Lane. The last stretch is narrow and uphill; allow time for arriving traffic.
