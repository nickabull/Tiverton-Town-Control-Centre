TIVVY DATA CENTRE · HANDOVER REFERENCE

Programme Studio User Guide

A working guide for colleagues covering the matchday programme. The Studio is currently a prototype; check the readiness warnings and do not treat it as the final 51-page publishing system.

Start an editionPage IntelligenceRed alignment guidesPhotographsLive football dataEditorial checksSaving & backupsHandover checklist

1. Choose the matchday edition

Under Running Order, select an existing edition or create a new one. Programme Designer changes only the selected edition. Template Designer is for deliberate changes to the master. Confirm opponent, date and kick-off before editing.

2. Each page asks for different information

The right panel has Content & Data, Design & Layout, and Checks & Approval. The cover expects the opponent, crest, date, kick-off, featured player and photograph. Latest News expects six stories and two credited photos. Squad has a separate player status and include in this edition checkbox. These prototype player changes do not yet change the live club website.

3. Red alignment guides

Select an object, open Design & Layout and move it with X/Y sliders. Within 5px of another element's left edge, centre or right edge (or top, middle and bottom), a red line appears and the object snaps into position. Uncheck Smart alignment guides & snapping or hold Alt for free movement.

Illustration of the red guides — not a live screenshot.

4. Photographs and credits

On the cover or Latest News page, choose Viv's photo library in Content & Data (or Design & Layout), then select a photo and placement. You can also upload an image. Always check copyright and the credit/byline before approving. Images uploaded into this browser prototype can fill local storage.

5. Live football data

Page 17 · Around the League uses the same Google Apps Script fixture feed as the club website widget, filtered to this edition's match date. Open Page 17 to load the fixtures. Check the data, then select Approve & freeze for print. Unlock only when corrections or updated results are needed. The website widget stays live; the approved programme snapshot does not. League Table and Results Matrix automation are not yet connected.

6. Fix editorial problems page by page

Open each page and watch for the small grey editorial warning. Click Review this page to open its findings and jump to the relevant text. Rules flag repeated words, basic typos, suspicious spacing, name variants and missing photo credits. Ignore warning is for intentional wording only. The complete Editorial Health Check in the left panel still lists issues across implemented pages. This is not a full grammar checker or a 51-page proofread.

7. Undo, restore and backup

Use Undo / Redo after adjustments. Create named restore points before larger editorial or design changes. Download a backup and the diagnostic log from the left panel. As of this version, saving is local to this browser; there is no central autosave and no automatic website player update. Do not clear browser data until you have exported a backup.

8. Before somebody else takes over

  1. Open the correct edition and confirm opposition, date and kick-off.
  2. Work through each page's Content & Data and Checks & Approval.
  3. Verify live football information and freeze Page 17 before print.
  4. Check player statuses and image credits against club-approved sources.
  5. Resolve editorial warnings and inspect the A4 layout at 100%.
  6. Create a restore point, download a backup and obtain final human approval before publishing.
